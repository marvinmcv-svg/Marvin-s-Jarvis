# Seasonal Campaigns

Seasonal campaigns are Maple Street Roasters' main engine for getting customers to do something other than their usual order. We run four anchor campaigns per year — Spring Bloom (March), Summer Cold Brew (June), Harvest (September), and Holiday (November–December) — each with a defined launch date, asset kit, and exit date. A campaign without a hard exit date is a campaign that quietly becomes wallpaper.

Campaign build blocks:
- A limited-time drink or two from the Seasonal Menu
- A dedicated hashtag and 8–12 pre-shot assets in Notion
- A four-week posting plan slotted into the Social Media Calendar
- An email blast timed to launch day and a mid-campaign nudge
- An in-cafe chalkboard and window decal refresh
- A sales target and a defined "win" metric (drinks sold, new sign-ups, etc.)

Each campaign is sized to its window. Spring Bloom is small (two drinks, two weeks) because March revenue is soft. Holiday is our largest and runs six weeks with three drink specials, a gifting push, and a merchandise bundle. Every campaign pairs a drink launch from the Seasonal Menu with a posting plan in the Social Media Calendar, and the two have to ship on the same day or the launch feels off. Every campaign gets a debrief doc within a week of close — what sold, what didn't, what we'd repeat. The debrief feeds next year's plan.

The campaigns always link back to a merchandise moment — see [[Merchandise-Catalog]] for how we pair limited-run merch with each seasonal launch. We do not discount seasonal drinks; the urgency comes from the calendar, not the price. Past campaigns that underperformed (looking at you, 2023 Spring Bloom) get a postmortem and a "do not repeat" note in the campaign archive.
