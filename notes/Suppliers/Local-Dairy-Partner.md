# Local Dairy Partner

Our dairy partner is Cream Ridge Dairy, a family-owned operation about 40 minutes outside the city. They deliver three times a week (Tuesday, Friday, Saturday) and have been our sole dairy supplier since 2020. The relationship matters more than the size of the line item — dairy is the ingredient most likely to derail a service if it's late or short, so reliability wins over price.

Standing weekly order:
- 18 gallons whole milk (the workhorse for espresso drinks)
- 6 gallons 2% milk (lower-volume customer requests)
- 4 gallons oat milk (barista edition, the Oatly tap)
- 2 gallons heavy cream (Seasonal Menu steamers and sauces)
- 12 quarts half-and-half (for drip coffee station)
- 6 pints yogurt (in-house pastry program, see Pastry Sourcing)

Cream Ridge invoices at Net-15 and passes through fuel surcharges when diesel spikes, which is why we never let those invoices age — the terms in Supplier Payment Terms reflect that. Their margin is thin and they've been honest about it, which has built trust over five years. We've never had to chase a late delivery, and the one time their truck broke down, the owner drove a van personally to make the Saturday drop.

Dairy volume is forecast two weeks ahead against the cafe sales forecast, and adjusted each week based on actual pour. The yogurt we pull for the in-house pastry program is coordinated with Pastry Sourcing so the bake schedule never runs dry mid-week. The forecast feeds the Cash Flow Forecast since dairy is a frequent, predictable outflow — see [[Cash-Flow-Forecast]] for how dairy deliveries land in the 13-week projection. Seasonal Menu drinks that need extra cream or a specialty dairy (like the spring steamer) are confirmed against the Cream Ridge delivery schedule before the menu launches.
