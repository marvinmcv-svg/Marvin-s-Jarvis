# Coffee Bean Importers

Coffee Bean Importers is our primary green coffee partner and has been since we opened in 2019. They're a mid-size importer based in Minneapolis with direct trade relationships in Brazil, Colombia, Guatemala, and Ethiopia — which conveniently maps to the four components of our Espresso Blend Recipe. We buy roughly 1,800 lb of green coffee per month through them, split across standing lots and seasonal spot buys.

Standing relationship details:
- Account manager: Daniela Reyes, reachable by cell and Slack
- Net-30 terms, 2% discount if paid within 10 days (see Supplier Payment Terms)
- Minimum order: 1 full bag (70kg / 154lb) per origin
- Samples shipped free before any new lot commitment
- Quarterly cupping at their Minneapolis lab (Helen attends in person)

We rely on Daniela for lot-level transparency. Every bag arrives with a farm or cooperative name, harvest year, processing method, and a cupping score. Lots scoring below 84 are not considered for the house blend; lots scoring 87+ sometimes get pulled for a single-origin retail offering. When a standing lot is winding down, Daniela flags it two weeks ahead so the head roaster can approve a substitution before we run dry.

Green coffee is the largest single line in our COGS, so the relationship gets a disproportionate amount of attention. The 2% early-pay discount is the most valuable term in our Supplier Payment Terms stack, and we take it on every invoice that qualifies. Helen reviews the quarterly cupping notes alongside sales data, and any change in our blend components triggers a fresh costed recipe review. The green coffee intake and lot rotation is governed by the Inventory Rotation discipline — see [[Inventory-Rotation]] for how lots are dated, staged, and pulled forward.
