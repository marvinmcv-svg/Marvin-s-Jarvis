# Packaging Vendors

Packaging at Maple Street Roasters is split across two vendors. We use Printed Cup Company for our branded hot and cold cups, and EcoPack Supply for lids, to-go containers, and mailers. The split keeps us from being single-source dependent and lets us negotiate each category on its own merits. Packaging is a deceptively large line item — roughly $4,200 per month across both vendors combined.

Standing SKU list:
- 12oz, 16oz, 20oz hot cups (Printed Cup Company, custom Maple Street print)
- 12oz, 16oz, 24oz cold cups (Printed Cup Company, clear)
- Matching lids for all cup sizes (EcoPack Supply)
- 8oz and 16oz to-go boxes for pastries (EcoPack Supply)
- Branded mailers and tissue for merchandise (EcoPack Supply)
- Custom stickers and loyalty stamp cards (Printed Cup Company)

The to-go pastry boxes are sized to the Pastry Sourcing lineup, so any change to our pastry format means a packaging SKU change too — the two catalogs move together. The mailers and tissue support our Merchandise Catalog and are stocked separately so a surge in online merchandise orders never cannibalizes cafe packaging. Both vendors invoice at Net-45 with a $750 minimum order, which we hit comfortably on a normal cycle. The terms are spelled out in Supplier Payment Terms — we deliberately negotiated the longer window because packaging volume is predictable and the vendors have never pushed back. We do a full packaging inventory count on the first Sunday of each month; a SKU below two weeks of cover triggers a reorder that day.

Printed Cup Company runs our custom print plates, and any design change (new logo, seasonal print) carries a $280 plate fee that we treat as a marketing expense rather than a packaging cost. Plate fees and large stock-up orders are timed against the Quarterly Taxes cadence so the cash outflow lands in a planned quarter — see [[Quarterly-Taxes]] for how packaging capex gets bucketed. Returns and damages are logged within 48 hours of delivery; both vendors are responsive but require photos.
