# Hiring Process

We hire deliberately and slowly at Maple Street Roasters — a bad hire costs us more than a slow hire. The process is owned by Helen with input from Marco for barista roles and from the head roaster for roastery roles. We post on our website, on a local hospitality board, and on Instagram; we do not use general-purpose job boards because they generate too much noise for a 12-person shop.

Hiring steps:
- Application via our website form (no resumes-only walk-ins accepted)
- Phone screen with Helen, 15 minutes, confirms availability and pay band
- In-person trial shift, paid 2 hours, working alongside Marco
- Reference check (two references, both within the last 18 months)
- Final interview with Helen and the relevant team lead
- Offer letter with start date and the Barista Training start week

The trial shift is the most important step. We're watching for pace, attitude, and whether the candidate can take direction in front of customers. A candidate who aces the interview but underwhelms on the trial shift does not get an offer — and we've made that call more than once. Once an offer is accepted, the start date is coordinated through Shift Scheduling so the new hire's first week lines up with a quiet period in the cafe.

We hire at $18.50/hour for barista roles, $20.50 for shift leads, plus tips (which averaged $6.20/hour in 2024). Every offer letter references the Barista Training start date so expectations are clear from day one. See [[Performance-Reviews]] for the 30/60/90 review cadence that follows the hire.
