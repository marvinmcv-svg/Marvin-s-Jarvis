# Shift Scheduling

Shifts at Maple Street Roasters are published two weeks in advance on a shared Google Calendar that everyone can subscribe to. Helen owns the master schedule, with Marco covering cafe-side requests and the head roaster covering roastery coverage. The schedule is built Sunday evening for the week starting 11 days later, which gives everyone time to swap before it locks.

Shift structure:
- Open: 5:45 AM–1:00 PM (2 baristas minimum, 3 on weekends)
- Mid: 10:00 AM–4:00 PM (1 barista, peaks with lunch rush)
- Close: 1:00 PM–9:30 PM (2 baristas, closer owns the floor after 8:00 PM)
- Roastery: 6:00 AM–2:00 PM Monday–Friday (head roaster plus one apprentice)

Coverage rules are strict. The opener must be a signed-off barista (see the Daily Opening Checklist), and the closer must have at least 90 days of solo experience. No trainee is ever scheduled to close, and no trainee is ever the only barista on a shift. Swaps require 48 hours' notice and approval from the relevant lead — last-minute swaps are the most common source of shift gaps.

Scheduling also has to account for training cohorts and for review cycles. New hires block out a slot in Barista Training during their first two weeks, and Performance Reviews are scheduled in slower weeks so coverage doesn't suffer. Anyone with recurring performance concerns gets a slightly lighter schedule until the review is complete — see [[Barista-Training]] for how training capacity is protected on the schedule. Closing Procedures get a final pass on the schedule to confirm a qualified closer is on every close.
