# Barista Training

Every new barista at Maple Street Roasters goes through a two-week structured training before they are allowed to solo on the espresso bar. Training is owned by our lead barista (Marco) and runs in cohorts of one to two trainees at a time so the cafe never carries two learners on the same shift. The curriculum is pinned in a binder behind the bar and mirrors the modules in our internal wiki.

Two-week training modules:
- Week 1: cafe orientation, safety, register, drip and pour-over
- Week 1 end: dialing in the Espresso Blend Recipe with Marco
- Week 2: milk steaming, latte art, drink build standards
- Week 2 mid: customer service standards and conflict de-escalation
- Week 2 end: supervised solo bar shift, sign-off checklist

The sign-off is non-negotiable. A trainee who can't consistently hit the Espresso Blend Recipe shot time (26 seconds, 18g in, 36g out) does not solo. We retrain rather than push someone onto the floor — bad habits on the bar are expensive to undo and customers notice. New hires from our Hiring Process arrive with the same two-week curriculum regardless of prior experience; we've found that prior cafe experience often means re-learning our specific standards.

Trainee shifts are slotted through Shift Scheduling so coverage stays stable during the training window — typically one trainee per shift with a senior barista as backup. Compensation during training is full hourly, not a training wage. We track training outcomes over time and feed the results into Performance Reviews so we can see whether the curriculum is actually producing capable baristas.
