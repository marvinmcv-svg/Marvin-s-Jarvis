# Quarterly Taxes

Maple Street Roasters files quarterly estimated federal and state taxes, with the bookkeeper closing the books five business days before each quarterly deadline. The Q4 payment for 2024 was $11,400 federal and $2,900 state, and we hold those funds in a dedicated tax savings account so the money is never in the operating account when the IRS draft hits.

Quarterly close checklist:
- Reconcile QuickBooks to bank and Square settlement reports
- Pull payroll summaries from Gusto for the quarter
- Apply depreciation entries for the Probat L5 and La Marzocco machines
- Compute sales tax owed across cafe and wholesale channels
- Review the monthly budget actuals against projection for the quarter
- Submit estimated payment via EFTPS and the state portal by the 15th

We use the prior year's safe-harbor figure as a floor and adjust upward when revenue is running ahead. The bookkeeper pulls the prior quarter's actuals from the Cash Flow Forecast archive to sanity-check that estimated payments track real profitability — if the forecast consistently underpredicts tax exposure, we revise the safe-harbor number mid-year rather than waiting for the annual true-up. The Monthly Budget also gets a quarterly close pass, since any line that ran hot all quarter changes both our estimated payment and our reserve target.

Quarterly Taxes are also when we revisit our entity structure. We're an LLC taxed as an S-corp, and Helen's reasonable-compensation figure gets re-evaluated each quarter against distributions. Any large capital purchase (like the new EK43 we're eyeing for Q3) is timed against the quarterly so we can model the Section 179 deduction before committing.
