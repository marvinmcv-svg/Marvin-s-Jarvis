# Cash Flow Forecast

The 13-week cash flow forecast is the single most-watched document at Maple Street Roasters. Helen and the bookkeeper rebuild it every Friday afternoon using actuals from the Square Register settlement, payroll runs, and any invoice postings from the prior seven days. The output is a rolling projection of the operating account balance for each of the next 13 Fridays.

Key inputs the forecast depends on:
- Daily card settlement averages pulled from Square (we use a 14-day rolling mean)
- Net-30 and Net-15 vendor outflows governed by Supplier Payment Terms
- Bi-weekly payroll and the monthly rent debit on the 1st
- Seasonal revenue swings (December rush, late-January lull)
- Reserve transfers for equipment and taxes

The forecast's job is to flag trouble two months before it hits. Our rule of thumb: if the projected balance on any Friday dips below $18,000, we freeze discretionary spend and pull marketing launches. If it dips below $10,000, Helen triggers a short-term line of credit draw. Vendor timing is governed by Supplier Payment Terms, so the outflow side of the forecast is only as accurate as our AP discipline — a late-paid invoice quietly distorts the next week's projection. The forecast is the live wire of the Monthly Budget — a budget can be balanced on a quarterly basis and still have a fatal week in the middle.

Inventory turns also matter here, since cash tied up in old green coffee lots is essentially frozen. See [[Inventory-Rotation]] for how we keep stock moving and protect working capital. The forecast is shared with the management team by Sunday evening so the week ahead opens with everyone aligned.
