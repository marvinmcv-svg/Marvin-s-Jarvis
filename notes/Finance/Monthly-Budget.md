# Monthly Budget

Maple Street Roasters runs a 12-month rolling budget that the owner (Helen) revises on the second Monday of each month. The budget is built bottom-up from category owners — green coffee, dairy, packaging, payroll, marketing — and reconciled against actuals in QuickBooks. The headline targets for January 2025 are revenue of $94,000 and total controllable expenses under $61,000, leaving a 35% gross margin before rent and depreciation.

Budget categories:
- Green coffee and roastery supplies: $16,500
- Cafe COGS (milk, syrups, pastries): $9,200
- Payroll (12 staff, hourly + salary): $24,800
- Rent and utilities: $6,900
- Marketing and loyalty: $1,800
- Equipment maintenance reserve: $1,800

The monthly budget feeds directly into the Cash Flow Forecast, which is what Helen actually steers by week to week — a budget that looks fine on paper can still drown you if the timing is off. We hold a 30-minute review each month where category owners explain any line over 8% variance. Persistent overruns get rolled into the Quarterly Taxes conversation because they change our estimated payments and our reserves.

Beyond the current year, the budget is the working layer of the Annual Roadmap. Any new hire, equipment purchase, or campaign commitment first has to find a line in the monthly budget before it's approved. See [[Annual-Roadmap]] for the three-year horizon that the monthly rolls up into.
