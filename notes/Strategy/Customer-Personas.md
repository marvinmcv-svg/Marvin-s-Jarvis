# Customer Personas

We plan all of our marketing and a fair amount of our product decisions against four customer personas. The personas were built in 2022 from a mix of register data, loyalty signups, and a 200-person customer survey, and they get revisited every January. They are not market segments in the abstract sense — each persona maps to a real person we know by name.

The four personas:
- "Daily Regular" — Marco's archetype, in 5 mornings a week, drip coffee, knows staff by name
- "Weekend Treat" — comes Saturday with a partner, specialty drink, pastry, lingers
- "Remote Worker" — weekday mid-morning, large drip, occupies a table for 2–3 hours
- "Wholesale Buyer" — local cafe or restaurant, buys 5–20lb of beans per week

Each persona has a defined primary need, a primary channel, and a defined "what we don't try to sell them" list. The Daily Regular gets the Loyalty Program and never gets upsold on specialty drinks. The Weekend Treat gets Seasonal Menu drinks and pastry pairings. The Remote Worker gets reliable wifi, a clean table, and a single polite check-in — never a sales push. The Wholesale Buyer gets a separate order flow and a quarterly check-in from Helen.

Every campaign, every post in the Social Media Calendar, and every Email Newsletter send is tagged with which persona it speaks to. We deliberately do not try to speak to all four every week — the calendar rotates focus so no persona is neglected and no persona is spammed. The personas are also the lens for the Annual Roadmap — see [[Annual-Roadmap]] for how persona growth projections shape which strategic initiatives get funded each year.
