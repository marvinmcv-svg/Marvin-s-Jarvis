# Annual Roadmap

The annual roadmap is Maple Street Roasters' three-year-looking strategic document, refreshed each December and reviewed quarterly. Helen owns the roadmap with input from the leads, and it sits above the Monthly Budget and Quarterly Taxes in our planning stack. The roadmap is not a wish list — every item on it has an owner, a budget envelope, and a defined checkpoint.

2025 roadmap themes:
- Open a second cafe location (target Q3 2025, contingent on site search)
- Grow wholesale green coffee accounts from 4 to 10
- Launch a subscription program (monthly bean delivery)
- Reduce single-use packaging by 30% by Q4 2025
- Add one roastery apprentice role (tied to Performance Reviews pipeline)

Each theme breaks into quarterly milestones. The second location, for example, has Q1 as site search and LOI, Q2 as lease and buildout budget, Q3 as construction and hiring, and Q4 as opening. The Monthly Budget carries the actual spend against each milestone, and Quarterly Taxes checkpoints confirm whether we're tracking to the planned cash position. A theme that misses two consecutive checkpoints gets descoped or deferred — we do not let a roadmap item quietly consume money without progress.

Customer Personas drive which roadmap themes get prioritized — the subscription program, for instance, exists because our most engaged persona asked for it. The roadmap is also where staff growth is planned: the apprentice role is contingent on the Performance Reviews pipeline producing an internal promotion to free a senior slot. Seasonal Campaigns are tactical, not strategic, but each campaign is checked against the roadmap to confirm it's supporting the year's themes — see [[Seasonal-Campaigns]] for how campaign calendars align with roadmap milestones.
