# Closing Procedures

Close at Maple Street Roasters runs from 8:00 PM to roughly 9:30 PM. The closer (usually Devon or Aisha) owns the floor from the last customer out the door until the alarm is armed. The close is the mirror image of the Daily Opening Checklist: every step that opens the cafe has a corresponding teardown, and a sloppy close guarantees a chaotic morning.

Close-of-day sequence:
- Lock front door at 8:00 PM, post "closed" sign, finish in-cafe tickets
- Pull drip urns, dump grounds, run hot water rinse cycle
- Backflush both espresso groups with cafiza, scrub portafilters
- Wipe down counters, pastry case glass, and the Probat L5 staging table
- Restock cups, lids, to-go boxes for morning opener
- Sweep, mop, take out recycling and compost

The closer counts the till in the back office, drops cash into the timed safe, and runs the Square Register Z-report. That Z-report feeds the daily sales entry that the manager reconciles against the Cash Flow Forecast each Tuesday — variance over $75 gets investigated before the next open. Green coffee pulled for tomorrow's roast is staged on the staging table and labeled with the lot number.

The last task is always the same: walk the floor with the closing photo checklist (12 photos), arm the ADT panel, and text the manager "cafe closed and secure." If you cannot complete any step — say, a grinder is acting up or the safe won't lock — do NOT arm the alarm until the manager acknowledges.
