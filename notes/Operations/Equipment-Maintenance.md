# Equipment Maintenance

Maple Street Roasters runs a tight, slightly aging equipment fleet that we baby ruthlessly. The Probat L5 roaster is the heart of the operation, flanked by a 2021 Mahlkönig EK43 grinder, two La Marzocco Linea PB espresso machines, and the workhorse Square Register POS terminals. Every piece has a maintenance card on the wall above it listing daily, weekly, and monthly tasks, plus the technician's direct cell.

Daily and weekly tasks:
- Espresso machines: backflush with cafiza each close, group gaskets inspected Mondays
- Grinders: burr brush and vacuum nightly, full burr inspection last Friday of each month
- Roaster: chaff cyclone emptied daily, exhaust probe calibration checked weekly
- Ice machine: sanitize and dump cycle every 14 days
- Refrigeration: coil vacuum and condenser wipe-down monthly

When the espresso machines drift, dialing in the Espresso Blend Recipe becomes a guessing game — so we lock machine performance before touching the recipe. We keep a $1,800 repair reserve on the books; anything beyond that gets flagged for capital review and surfaces during Quarterly Taxes planning, since the La Marzocco warranty terms and depreciation schedule both feed the accountant's work.

Major service intervals are scheduled by the head roaster into the calendar that drives the Daily Opening Checklist, so morning staff know if a technician visit will overlap their shift. If a machine goes down mid-service, the protocol is: tag it out, route orders to the second machine, and text the on-call technician within five minutes. Never attempt internal repairs during service hours. See [[Daily-Opening-Checklist]] for how maintenance windows appear on the morning routine.
