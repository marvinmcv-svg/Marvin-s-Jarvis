# Espresso Blend Recipe

Our house espresso blend, "Maple Street No. 4," is a four-component blend designed to hit a chocolate-forward, medium-body shot that holds up in milk. The recipe has been stable since spring 2023, with minor tweaks only when a component lot changes character. All four components are sourced through Coffee Bean Importers, which simplifies logistics and keeps our green coffee relationship consolidated with one accountable partner. The target shot is 18g in, 36g out, in 26 seconds, pulled on the La Marzocco Linea PB at 9.2 bar.

Blend components (by weight, green):
- 40% Brazil Cerrado, natural process
- 25% Colombia Huila, washed
- 20% Guatemala Antigua, washed
- 15% Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, natural (for aromatics)

Each component is roasted separately on the Probat L5 to its own profile, then rested 48 hours before blending. The Brazil gets a darker development (end at 425°F, just into second crack), while the Ethiopia stays light (end at 405°F, just past first crack). Blend ratios are weighed on the 5kg scale to ±10g tolerance; sloppy blending is the most common reason a batch tastes off.

Dialing in is a daily ritual. The opener pulls the first shot at 6:15 AM and adjusts the EK43 grind in 0.2 increments until the shot time lands between 25 and 27 seconds. A blend change is never made unilaterally — Marco and the head roaster agree before any ratio shifts, and the old recipe is archived with a tasting note. Seasonal Menu drinks use the same base espresso unless a specific campaign calls for a single-origin substitute. Equipment issues that affect extraction are addressed before any recipe change — see [[Equipment-Maintenance]] for the calibration sequence.
