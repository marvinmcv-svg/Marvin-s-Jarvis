# Pastry Sourcing

Our pastries come from two local bakery partners plus an in-house baked-goods program for a small set of items. The split is deliberate: we get reliability and volume from the partners, and we get a signature offering from the in-house program. About 65% of pastry sales by units come from partners, and the in-house items account for the higher-margin 35% by revenue.

Pastry lineup and sources:
- Almond croissant, plain croissant, pain au chocolat — Louve Bakery (delivered Tue/Fri)
- Vegan banana bread, gluten-free brownie — in-house (baked Mon/Thu)
- Seasonal savory galette — Louve Bakery (rotates with the Seasonal Menu)
- Morning bun and maple scone — Pioneer Pastry (delivered Wed/Sat)
- Mini financier (holiday only) — in-house

We order against a forecast that combines the prior four weeks of sales with any campaign or seasonal lift. Over-ordering pastries is the single most common waste line in our COGS — a tray of unsold almond croissants is roughly $42 in lost margin. The forecast is reviewed each Monday with the cafe lead so the Tuesday order reflects the actual weekend pull.

Pastry inventory is rotated FIFO with the dairy case, and the Local Dairy Partner delivery is timed to land the same morning so the case is fully stocked before open. Packaging for to-go pastries (windowed bags, parchment, labels) comes from our Packaging Vendors and is stocked in a separate dry-storage shelf. See [[Seasonal-Menu]] for how the seasonal galette and any campaign-specific pastry get added to the standing order.
