# Seasonal Menu

The seasonal menu at Maple Street Roasters rotates four times a year and is the main way we keep the cafe experience fresh without constantly changing our core offering. Each seasonal menu runs roughly 10–12 weeks and features two specialty drinks, one non-coffee option, and a rotating syrup made in-house. The core menu (drip, espresso drinks, pour-over) stays put.

Spring 2025 seasonal menu:
- "Maple Cardamom Latte" — house espresso, maple syrup, cardamom tincture, oat milk option
- "Cold Brew Tonic" — cold brew, tonic, dehydrated orange wheel
- "Honey Chamomile Steamer" (non-coffee)
- House-made cardamom-vanilla syrup (replaces winter's gingerbread)

Every drink on the seasonal menu is costed to a target of 22% COGS or lower, and the costing sheet is signed off by Helen before launch. Drinks are built on the Espresso Blend Recipe unless a single-origin substitute is justified — and we rarely justify it, since consistency in the base shot is a customer expectation we don't want to break. Pairings with Pastry Sourcing are planned at the same time so the seasonal croissant (this spring: pistachio-cardamom) lands on the same launch date.

The seasonal menu is the product anchor for Seasonal Campaigns, so the menu launch date drives the campaign launch date, not the other way around. Ingredients that require a dairy partner delivery (the heavy cream for the steamer, for instance) are confirmed against the Local Dairy Partner delivery schedule before the menu is finalized.
