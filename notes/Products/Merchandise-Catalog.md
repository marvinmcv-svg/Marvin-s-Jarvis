# Merchandise Catalog

The merchandise catalog at Maple Street Roasters is intentionally small — about 12 SKUs at any given time, rotating seasonally. Merchandise is roughly 6% of revenue but a much larger share of margin and brand presence, so we treat it as a marketing line as much as a product line. Every SKU has to justify its shelf space within 90 days or it gets rotated out.

Standing catalog:
- 12oz and 1lb bags of house blend and single-origin beans
- Branded ceramic mug (two colors, charcoal and cream)
- Canvas tote with our roastery line drawing
- French press (Bodum Brazil, branded box)
- Porcelain espresso cups (set of two)
- Brewing scale (0.1g resolution, branded)
- Sticker pack and enamel pin (low-price impulse items)

Seasonal items — a holiday mug, a spring tote refresh — get added three to four weeks ahead of their Seasonal Campaigns launch and pulled within two weeks of the campaign ending. We do not let seasonal merch linger; it dates the brand. Packaging for online and to-go merchandise orders (mailers, tissue, branded stickers) is sourced through our Packaging Vendors and restocked every two weeks.

The catalog is photographed twice a year for the Social Media Calendar, and the asset set lives in the same Notion folder as the campaign assets. Loyalty Program redemptions for merchandise (the 350-star tier) are tracked separately from paid sales so we can see whether the catalog is doing its job as a reward as well as a revenue line.
